# Hollow Knight: Silksong — Complete 100% Walkthrough

You can reach **100% and see the true ending on one save**. Follow the three acts in order, mark off the collections as you go, and check the in-game percentage before fighting Lost Lace. The route contains boss and ending spoilers. Set the Steam achievement detours aside if you are only aiming for the percentage on your save. [What counts toward 100%](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Completion_%28Silksong%29) · [Steam achievements](https://steamcommunity.com/achievements/1030300)

## Act 1 — Reach the Citadel

1. **Moss Grotto → Bone Bottom → The Marrow.** Defeat Moss Mother, climb through the chapel and read the Bone Bottom wish board. Buy Shakra's quill and Compass when you can afford them. Pick up the Shard Pendant and Straight Pin in The Marrow, and learn **Silkspear** in Mosshome. Cut the Bell Beast free with Silkspear, defeat it and complete its memory for the first **Silk Heart**. Open the Bellway and ring the region's bell.
2. **Deep Docks.** Unlock the lift shortcut and get **Swift Step**. Fight **Lace** in the docks now if you want **Pharloom’s Welcome**. Ring the bell by the Far Fields exit. Buy the first Crafting Kit and Magma Bell from Forge Daughter. Return with Craftmetal for Sting Shard.
3. **Far Fields.** Complete the Seamstress's **Flexile Spines** wish for **Drifter's Cloak**. Ride the updrafts, then come back for **Fourth Chorus**. The fight earns **Servant** and opens the way to the second Savage Beastfly hunt in Act 2. Visit Mort at Pilgrim’s Rest for a Tool Pouch and Weighted Belt.
4. **Greymoor.** Speak to Nuu at Halfway Home and defeat new species as you travel. Clear the eastern bird arena to reach the bell and **Thread Storm**. In the west, visit the **Chapel of the Reaper** for its crest and continue to Bellhart. If moving the flea caravan bypassed Moorwing, look for it later outside Greymoor’s Bellway.
5. **Shellwood → Bellhart.** Defeat Sister Splinter, learn **Cling Grip** and ring the Shellwood bell. Wall-jump into the upper entrance to Bellhart. Defeat **Widow** and bind her power to learn **Needolin**. Ring Bellhart's bell, talk to Pavo, then take your needle to **Plinney** for its first upgrade.
6. **Backtrack with Cling Grip.** Take the upper route from Moss Grotto to the **Chapel of the Wanderer**. In Hunter’s March, defeat the Savage Beastfly at the **Chapel of the Beast** for the Beast Crest. Return early in Act 2 if you need more health or damage. Start Greyroot’s **Rite of the Pollip** in Shellwood, and open the Bellhart wish board.
7. **Blasted Steps.** Check the five bells in The Marrow, Deep Docks, Greymoor, Bellhart and Shellwood. Visit Pinstress in her hanging house for **Needle Strike**. Talk to the pilgrims at the **Grand Gate**, play the Needolin and defeat **Last Judge**. Equip the Magma Bell for fire protection and step away after the final hit. Open the gate and enter the Citadel. [Act 1 video route](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LbhgEBA6Oxk) · [Grand Gate requirements](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Grand_Gate)

**First five fleas.** Save the one near the caravan in The Marrow, the two near Deep Docks’ Bellway and upper passages, and one each in Hunter’s March and Far Fields. Talk to Mooshka once you have five to move the caravan to Greymoor. Speak to Grishkin there for **Flea Brew**. [Flea locations](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/hollow-knight-silksong-all-lost-flea-locations)

## Act 2 — Open the map and prepare the endings

### Get Clawline and the double jump

1. Collect the Spool Fragment above the **Grand Gate** scales. Continue into the **Choral Chambers**, open the Grand Bellway and rest at **Songclave**. Ring the First Shrine to bring in its residents and grab the **White Key** from the passage to the right.
2. Defeat the **Cogwork Dancers** and descend through the Cogwork Core. Enter the **Whispering Vaults** and use the lift near The Stage to reach the **Underworks**. The Whiteward entrance is another route if you have the White Key. Get **Clawline** at the **Cauldron** and open the shortcut to Twelfth Architect.
3. From the Citadel, reach **The Slab**, unlock its Bellway and cross to **Mount Fay**. Rest at the benches, get the **Memory Crystal** behind the wall near the lower bench and climb with Cling Grip and Clawline. Bind the **Faydown Cloak** at the summit to unlock the double jump.
4. Return to the Citadel and enter **Memorium** from around the Cogwork Core. Take the eastern exterior route into **Putrified Ducts** and unlock its Bellway. Recruit **Vog** to the caravan so she can mark remaining fleas. Find **Pale Lake** and tell Mooshka about it. Talk to him after **12 fleas** and the Grand Gate for a Spool Fragment. At **22**, move the caravan to Pale Lake and collect the Tool Pouch at **Fleatopia**. ### Rosaries, keys and upgrades

- **Rosaries.** From Songclave’s bench, go left and climb to the long hall with three **Grand Reeds**. Bait their first swing, get behind them and punish the recovery. Rest at the bench to reset the hall. Equip **Thief’s Mark** when you can clear it reliably. [Songclave farm](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/silksong-rosary-farm/)
- **Simple Keys and Craftmetal.** Set aside a key for the High Halls bank and its Rosary Cannon. Open Wormways and, for the Verdania achievement, the Green Prince’s cell in Sinner’s Road. Keys come from Pebb, Jubilana, a Roachkeeper in northeast Sinner’s Road and Sands of Karak. Enter the lower docks from Far Fields with Clawline to save a key there. Eight Craftmetals cover your tools, including your choice of one Silkshot. [Key locations](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Simple_Key_%28Silksong%29) · [Craftmetal locations](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Craftmetal)
- **Two Pale Oils in Act 2.** Get the first in the Whispering Vaults. For the other, start **Great Taste of Pharloom** with Mergwin in the Choral Chambers. Bring him the Moss Druid’s stew, Creige’s nectar from Halfway Home, the Crustnut from Karak, Lugoli’s catch, and Tipp and Pill’s meat delivery. Open shortcuts and rehearse the walk between the Citadel and Bellhart before taking the fragile delivery. Carry it on foot from Bellhart straight to Mergwin. Take both oils to Plinney and save **450 Rosaries for the third needle upgrade**. [Ingredient routes](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Great_Taste_of_Pharloom) · [Pale Oils](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/silksong-pale-oil-needle-upgrade/)
- **Sylphsong.** Use Memory Lockets on crests other than **Hunter**. Eva counts their open slots, including Silk Skill slots, and the ones you unlock. At **32 slots**, return to Eva in Weavenest Atla, exhaust her dialogue and bind to get **Sylphsong**. If you are short, come back after unlocking and upgrading the Shaman Crest in Act 3. [Eva’s thresholds and Memory Locket locations](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Memory_Locket)

### Assemble the Threefold Melody and unlock Silk and Soul

1. Learn the **Conductor’s Melody** after the High Halls arena, the **Vaultkeeper’s Melody** from Cardinius in the Whispering Vaults and the **Architect’s Melody** at the Cogwork Core mechanism. Play the **Threefold Melody** before the statues in the heart of the Citadel. Climb into **The Cradle**, beat **Lace** and finish her memory to receive the third Silk Heart.
2. Move the caravan to **Fleatopia** and get **Faydown Cloak** if you skipped Mount Fay. Complete **Restoration of Bellhart** and **Bellhart’s Glory**, then get the **Bellhome** key from Pavo.
3. Finish the six required wishes: **Flexile Spines**, **A Lifesaving Bridge**, **Crawbug Clearing**, **Strengthening Songclave**, **Trail’s End** and **Balm for the Wounded**. Complete **Bone Bottom Repairs** and **Building Up Songclave** to open the bridge and Songclave wishes. Buy any Shakra maps you missed from her Bellhart stall.
4. You also need **17 wish points**. One route is **Berry Picking**, **An Icon of Hope**, **Rite of the Pollip**, **Volatile Flintbeetles**, **The Terrible Tyrant**, **Garb of the Pilgrims**, **My Missing Courier**, **My Missing Brother**, **Pinmaster’s Oil**, **Silver Bells**, **Savage Beastfly**, **Bugs of Pharloom**, **Roach Guts**, **Great Taste of Pharloom**, **The Lost Merchant**, **The Wandering Merchant** and **Fine Pins**. Substitute **Cloaks of the Choir**, **Alchemist’s Assistant**, **Broodfeast** or **Final Audience** if needed. Each of these wishes is worth one point. The first run of each delivery is worth half a point.
5. Accept **Silk and Soul** at Bellhart or from the Caretaker in Songclave. Collect the souls from **Chapel Maid** in Bone Bottom, **Bell Hermit** below Bellhart and the hut after **Groal** in Bilewater. Take the **Snare Setter** from the hidden workshop in **Weavenest Atla** and return to the Caretaker to prepare the **Soul Snare**. [Wish points and requirements](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Silk_and_Soul)

### Finish Act 2 in this order

Follow all four steps to earn the ending achievements. For the in-game percentage alone, start with the Witch Crest and then proceed to Snared Silk.

1. **Weaver Queen.** Beat **Grand Mother Silk** without the curse and choose **Bind**. Watch the credits and reload the save to resume before the fight.
2. **Twisted Child.** Once you have access to the boss and an upgraded needle, take the **Twisted Bud** to Greyroot after her six pollips. Accept **Rite of Rebirth** and defeat Grand Mother Silk while cursed. With reduced Silk and no healing or tools, stay grounded when you can, dodge first and attack after her longer moves.
3. **Witch Crest.** Reload the save and start **Infestation Operation** with **Yarnaby** in Greymoor. Buy **Steel Spines** from Crull and Benjin in Sinner’s Road and give them to Yarnaby. The treatment awards the **Witch Crest** and closes the Twisted Child ending on this save.
4. **Snared Silk.** With **Silk and Soul** complete, defeat Grand Mother Silk again and play the **Needolin** while she is down. After the credits, load your save and break the black threads to enter Act 3. [Ending footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3uBMMcN7kBw&t=2947s) · [Twisted Bud and Yarnaby route](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/silksong-twisted-bud-infestation-operation/)

## Act 3 — The final seven points and the true ending

### Reveal your completion percentage

1. Descend from **The Cradle**, fight through the Void enemies and return to the Citadel station. Defeat **Bell Eater** and collect **Beastling Call**. Speak to the shamans at the **Ruined Chapel** below Bone Bottom.
2. Speak to **Forge Daughter** and **Ballow** in Deep Docks, then meet him at the **Diving Bell** for the **Bell Key**. Use the shortcut from the Forebrothers Signis & Gron arena and descend into **The Abyss**. Play the Needolin at **Weavenest Absolom**, bind **Silk Soar**, and pick up **Farsight** in the upper eastern passage before leaving.
3. Assemble **Farsight** in **Bellhome** in Bellhart. The completion percentage is now visible in the menu and on the save. Return to the shamans with Silk Soar for **Elegy of the Deep**, then use the hidden path above their room to get the **Shaman Crest**. [Abyss video route](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdDlNkKWZzE) · [Farsight location](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Farsight)

### Clean up the Act 3 exclusives

- [ ] **Pale Nails.** Use Silk Soar to return to the top of The Cradle and bind the arm of Grand Mother Silk.
- [ ] **Pin Badge and Brightvein Mask Shard.** Take Pinstress’s duel wish in Bellhart after learning Needle Strike. Read her note at Blasted Steps. Use Silk Soar above the lake on Mount Fay and complete **Brightvein** for its shard. Back on the summit route, wake Pinstress with a needle strike and beat her for the **Pin Badge**.
- [ ] **Far Fields Mask Shards.** From Hunter’s March, use Silk Soar at Karmelita’s statue. Go east and beat **Sprintmaster Swift** in all three **Fastest in Pharloom** races. Reach Karmelita to unlock **The Hidden Hunter** at Bellhart. Defeat **Gurr the Outcast**, grab the grass doll from the next room and turn it in for another shard.
- [ ] **Dark Hearts Mask Shard.** Take the Bellhart wish after exploring The Abyss and opening the ending route. Destroy 12 of the large stationary **Void Masses** as you travel and turn in the wish. [Void Mass route](https://silksongwiki.com/walkthrough/steel-soul/#act-3-cleanup)
- [ ] **Final Pale Oil.** Rescue all 30 fleas, including **Kratt**, **Vog** and the **Huge Flea**, then talk to Mooshka for the **Egg of Flealia**. Start **Ecstasy of the End** and beat the three festival records. For juggling, use longer reach to keep the fleas grouped on one side. In the dodge event, move only when a flea is about to hit. For bouncing, the Wanderer Crest gives you a reliable down strike. Claim the oil from Mooshka.
- [ ] **Pale Steel Needle.** If Plinney is missing, rescue him near the Shellwood Bellway. Bring the final Pale Oil and **680 Rosaries** to his shop. [Festival route and Plinney](https://silksongwiki.com/walkthrough/steel-soul/#100-fleatopia)

### Earn the Everbloom and finish at 100%

1. Use **Elegy of the Deep** to enter the old memories and win **three of four** fights. This route uses **Skarrsinger Karmelita** above Far Fields, **Nyleth** in the Grand Gate shrine past Shrine Guardian Seth, and **Crust King Khann** in the Coral Tower in Sands of Karak. For the fourth, enter **Verdania** from Putrified Ducts to fight the **Clover Dancers**. Do all four for their individual achievements. [Video routes for all four memories](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gA3q2YOsIDA)
2. Return three hearts to the shamans at the Ruined Chapel. Complete **Red Memory** to receive the **Everbloom**, worth 1%.
3. **Check Farsight.** If you are at 99%, start with all 51 tools and six Silk Skills, then check Sylphsong, both sets of equipment upgrades, three Silk Hearts and all ten masks. Incomplete mask and spool sets give no percentage.
4. At **100%**, return to The Abyss and approach Grand Mother Silk’s cocoon. Defeat **Lost Lace**. Keep enough Silk to heal and take short punish windows after her attack strings. Watch **Sister of the Void** to earn **Completion** with your checked percentage. [True ending fight and credits](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3uBMMcN7kBw)

## If you also want all Steam achievements

- **Main save.** Beat Lace in Deep Docks, Fourth Chorus and all four ancient bosses. Follow the Act 2 ending order above. For **Hero’s Call**, take the Bellhart wish after Everbloom or three Act 3 Garmond and Zaza encounters, then follow Zaza down and west from the Blasted Steps Bellway. For **True Hunter**, complete the 230 required Hunter’s Journal entries in Standard mode and return to Nuu. For **Cartographer**, get Shakra’s maps and those of the Citadel; open Bellways and Ventrica stations as you pass through. [Achievement requirements](https://steamcommunity.com/achievements/1030300) · [Hero’s Call](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Hero%27s_Call) · [Hunter’s Journal](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Hunter%27s_Journal_%28Silksong%29)
- **Passing of the Age.** Use Silk Soar to find the tablet above Pale Lake. Follow Mister Mushroom in order: Moss Grotto, above Bone Bottom, Far Fields, Greymoor, The Slab, Mount Fay’s summit, above The Cradle. Play the Needolin and exhaust his dialogue at each stop. Finish the game and watch the post-credits scene. [Seven encounters on video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G8RPYSwYhpM)
- **Speedrunner.** Finish an ending in **under five hours**. On a dedicated save, prioritise movement, needle upgrades and the Threefold Melody, then beat Grand Mother Silk and choose Bind. Practise the sections on your main save. [4h02 run without major skips](https://www.speedrun.com/silksong/runs/mep99p3y)
- **Speed Completion.** Reach **100%** and finish an ending in **under 30 hours**. Follow the three-act route, skip optional endings you already earned and batch shopping with the Songclave Rosary loop. This can overlap with Steel Heart.
- **Steel Soul and Steel Heart.** Start a **Steel Soul** save. To earn both together, follow the Act 3 route, verify 100% before Lost Lace and finish. Prioritise masks, Plinney’s first upgrade and benches before dangerous fights. [Full Steel Soul run with chapters](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pa3Fl9Vy5R8)
