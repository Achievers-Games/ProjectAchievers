# Hollow Knight — 112% and All Steam Achievements

This route uses a normal save for **112%**, a **Steel Soul** save for 100%, and a third save for the five-hour run. The **Pantheon of Hallownest** is a separate achievement and usually takes the most practice. Times for the speedrun achievements come from the save file's in-game timer.

## Plan the choices before starting

- On the normal save, rescue Zote, finish the Grimm ritual and spare the Nailsmith. On the Steel Soul save, leave Zote behind, banish the troupe and grant the Nailsmith's request. Each pair of choices is exclusive on a single save.
- After the credits, reloading puts you back before the final fight. Do **The Hollow Knight** ending before obtaining **Void Heart**, which closes that ending on the save.
- The Seer asks for 600 Essence for **Attunement**, 1,800 for **Awakening** and 2,400 for **Ascension**.

## Save 1 — The normal run

### Open the main routes and defeat the bosses

Start in the Forgotten Crossroads, clear Greenpath for **Mothwing Cloak** and travel through Fungal Wastes for **Mantis Claw**. Open the City of Tears with the City Crest, get the first nail upgrade and learn **Desolate Dive** from Soul Master. Use it at the shaky floor in the Crossroads to enter Crystal Peak, where **Crystal Heart** lets you cross long gaps. Follow the list as you open the remaining areas.

- [ ] **Falsehood.** Defeat False Knight in the Forgotten Crossroads.
- [ ] **Charmed.** Get your first charm. Salubra sells several in the Crossroads.
- [ ] **Test of Resolve.** Defeat Hornet in Greenpath and collect Mothwing Cloak.
- [ ] **Respect.** Defeat the Mantis Lords in Mantis Village, below Fungal Wastes. Rest at the nearby bench first.
- [ ] **Illumination.** Defeat Soul Master at the Soul Sanctum in the City of Tears. Open the shortcut to the arena before trying again.
- [ ] **Honour.** Use a Simple Key at the City of Tears hatch and defeat Dung Defender in Royal Waterways.
- [ ] **Release.** Defeat Broken Vessel in Ancient Basin and continue for Monarch Wings.
- [ ] **Obsession.** Pick up the Love Key in Queen’s Gardens and unlock the Tower of Love from the Kingdom’s Edge approach. Defeat the Collector.
- [ ] **Execution.** Use Shade Cloak through the Shade Gate in Queen’s Gardens and defeat Traitor Lord. Dash through his shockwaves and fight at close range.
- [ ] **Proof of Resolve.** Defeat Hornet in Kingdom’s Edge. Beyond her arena, take the King’s Brand, which opens the Abyss beneath Ancient Basin. Get Shade Cloak there before attempting Traitor Lord.

### Dreamers and Essence

- [ ] Collect the **Dream Nail** in Resting Grounds and speak to the Seer.
- [ ] **Teacher.** Reach Monomon at the Teacher’s Archives in Fog Canyon. Speak to Quirrel before using the Dream Nail on Monomon.
- [ ] **Watcher.** Defeat the Watcher Knights and reach Lurien at the top of Watcher’s Spire in the City of Tears. Break the ceiling above the corridor to drop the chandelier on one knight beforehand.
- [ ] **Beast.** Reach Herrah in the Beast’s Den at Distant Village in Deepnest. Take the Stag Station before entering the den.
- [ ] **Witness.** After Monomon, find Quirrel at Blue Lake and sit beside him.
- [ ] **Attunement, Awakening and Ascension.** Collect 600, 1,800 and 2,400 Essence from Warrior Dreams, Dream Bosses and Whispering Roots. Visit the Seer at each threshold. The 1,800 reward awakens the Dream Nail.
- [ ] **Strength, Mortality and Peace.** Use the Dream Nail on False Knight, Soul Master and Broken Vessel after their normal fights. Beat Failed Champion, Soul Tyrant and Lost Kin for their respective achievements and Essence.
- [ ] **Memory.** After all three Dreamers, break the floor under the switch beside Dung Defender’s arena. Dream Nail the sleeping Dung Defender and defeat White Defender.

### Save Zote and complete the Grimm ritual

- [ ] **Rescue Zote.** Save him from Vengefly King in Greenpath, then free him from the webs in Deepnest.
- [ ] **Warrior and Rivalry.** Complete the Trial of the Warrior at the Colosseum of Fools in Kingdom’s Edge. Zote is the final opponent after both rescues.
- [ ] **Conqueror.** Complete the Trial of the Conqueror. Spend the reward Pale Ore on a nail upgrade.
- [ ] **Fool.** Complete the Trial of the Fool. Practise holding a Nail Art charge through the wall-only waves so you can hit flying enemies as soon as they appear.
- [ ] **Dark Romance.** Rescue Bretta in Fungal Wastes. After Zote returns from the Colosseum, Dream Nail his statue in Bretta’s basement in Dirtmouth and defeat Grey Prince Zote.
- [ ] **Grand Performance.** Light the Nightmare Lantern in Howling Cliffs. Equip Grimmchild to track the flames, then defeat Troupe Master Grimm in his Dirtmouth tent.
- [ ] **Ritual.** Collect the remaining flames and defeat Nightmare King Grimm. Save Brumm’s banishment for the next run.

### Nailsmith and side quests

- [ ] **Happy Couple.** Upgrade to Pure Nail. When the Nailsmith asks you to strike him, leave the area and visit Sheo’s hut in Greenpath to find them together. Save **Purity** for the next run.
- [ ] **Solace.** Take the Delicate Flower from Grey Mourner in Resting Grounds to the grave in Queen’s Gardens without taking damage. Clear the route before collecting the flower and avoid sitting at a bench on the return to Grey Mourner so the larger enemies stay dead.
- [ ] **Passing of the Age.** Equip Spore Shroom and speak to Mister Mushroom at all seven spots in order. Finish the game and watch the post-credits scene. [Mister Mushroom route](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Mister_Mushroom)

### Collections

- [ ] **Grubfriend and Metamorphosis.** Free 23, then 46 Grubs and return to Grubfather. Pick up the Collector’s Map upstairs in the Tower of Love after the fight and use it to find the remaining jars.
- [ ] **Protected and Masked.** Collect four, then all 16 Mask Shards.
- [ ] **Soulful and Worldsoul.** Collect three, then all nine Vessel Fragments.
- [ ] **Enchanted and Blessed.** Collect half the charms, then finish the collection and buy Salubra’s Blessing.
- [ ] **Connection and Hope.** Open half the Stag Stations, then all of them and visit Stag Nest.
- [ ] **Cartographer.** Buy each region map from Cornifer or from Iselda in Dirtmouth after Cornifer moves on.
- [ ] **Keen Hunter and True Hunter.** Fill out the Hunter’s Journal, complete the kill counts for the required entries and return to the Hunter in Greenpath for the Hunter’s Mark. Keep the journal open between passes when hunting common enemies.

### Endings

- [ ] **The Hollow Knight.** After the three Dreamers and before obtaining Void Heart, defeat the Hollow Knight in the Temple of the Black Egg.
- [ ] **Void.** Get a Kingsoul half from the White Lady in Queen’s Gardens. For the other, Dream Nail the Kingsmould corpse at Palace Grounds using the awakened Dream Nail and finish White Palace. Equip Kingsoul and descend into the Abyss to reach the Birthplace and obtain Void Heart.
- [ ] **Sealed Siblings.** Return to the Black Egg with Void Heart. When Hornet pins the Hollow Knight, keep attacking with the nail to finish the fight.
- [ ] **Dream No More.** Reload the save, repeat the fight and use the Dream Nail when Hornet pins the Hollow Knight. Defeat the Radiance.
- [ ] **Completion.** Finish any ending at 100% or above. Inspect the tablet near the Black Egg bench to reveal the completion percentage.

### Godhome and 112%

Take the breakable route in Royal Waterways to **Junk Pit**. Use a Simple Key on the Godseeker’s cocoon, then use the Dream Nail to enter **Godhome**. Practise each troublesome boss in the Hall of Gods before attempting a full Pantheon.

- [ ] **Brotherhood, Inspiration, Focus and Soul & Shade.** Complete the Pantheons of the Master, Artist, Sage and Knight in order.
- [ ] **Embrace the Void.** Complete the Pantheon of Hallownest. Practise Pure Vessel and Absolute Radiance separately once they are available in the Hall of Gods.
- [ ] **Pure Completion.** Check the save shows 112%, then finish an ending. The first four Pantheons count toward the percentage. Use the percentage table to find any missing bosses, abilities, charms or upgrades. [112% breakdown](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Completion_%28Hollow_Knight%29)

## Save 2 — Steel Soul at 100%

An ordinary death ends the run. The usual Dream Bosses and Godhome are safe to fail, while **White Palace, the Radiance and the Lifeblood Core room** can end the save. Budget your 100% with the completion table and take the basic Black Egg ending to avoid those fights and routes. Prioritise nail upgrades, Mask Shards and benches. [Steel Soul rules](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Steel_Soul_Mode_%28Hollow_Knight%29)

- [ ] **Neglect.** Leave Zote with Vengefly King in Greenpath. Return after obtaining Mantis Claw and strike his shell.
- [ ] **Banishment.** Defeat Troupe Master Grimm, meet Brumm at Distant Village, then return to the Nightmare Lantern. Break it before leaving the room.
- [ ] **Purity.** Upgrade to Pure Nail and kill the Nailsmith when he asks.
- [ ] **Steel Soul and Steel Heart.** Use the collection routes from the first save to reach at least 100%, verify the percentage at the Black Egg and beat the Hollow Knight.
- [ ] **Speed Completion.** Finish with at least 100% in under 20 in-game hours. Earn it here if you are on pace, or use a separate normal save for this target.

## Save 3 — Finish in under five hours

- [ ] **Speedrun 1 and Speedrun 2.** Both unlock if you finish within five in-game hours. Use the basic ending. Prioritise movement abilities, the Dream Nail and the three Dreamers. Practise the route below in segments, particularly the Watcher Knights fight.
